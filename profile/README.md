Domain Flock is a domain portfolio manager that helps you keep track of every domain you own, in one place.

Add your domains, whichever registrar they're with, and see them together: registration expiry dates, certificate (SSL) expiry dates, and the issues that need your attention. Organize them into groups, and invite teammates with the access they need.

### What we check

Depending on how you use each domain, we check:

- Whether the domain is reachable
- SSL certificate expiry
- Website safety settings
- Email protection (DMARC) and DNS
- Blacklist listings

When something needs attention, we alert you by email, or send it to another service with a webhook. Every issue comes with an explanation and steps to fix it.

*Made for people who own more domains than they can hold in their head.*
